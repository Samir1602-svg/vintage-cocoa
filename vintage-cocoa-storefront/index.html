Why Connoisseurs Choose Vintage Cocoa

Unlike mass-produced compound chocolates loaded with palm oil and refined wax, every Vintage Cocoa creation is crafted from pure single-origin cacao butter.

01

Single-Origin Estate Harvesting

Ethically sourced Forastero & Trinitario cacao pods from Idukki & Malabar estates, fermented for 6 days to unlock deep fruity and nutty notes.

02

48-Hour Granite Stone Conching

Slow-ground under granite wheels for 48 hours to achieve a microscopic 18-micron silkiness that melts effortlessly at body temperature.

03

Hand-Tempered & Gold-Leaf Finished

Marble-slab tempered by master chocolatiers for that signature crisp "snap" and glossy mirror sheen, finished with roasted nuts and edible gold.
